Layers: background, globe (halo, orb, land-dots, live-market-dots), leaders, pins, cards (card-lagos, card-dakar, card-accra, card-abidjan).
Live markets (Nigeria, Ghana and the eight XOF countries) are blue dots. Text is live text in JetBrains Mono.
Flags are simple vector drawings. Cards show city, country and currency only, with no amounts.

Colours: dots #76797F, live-market dots #1261F2, currency text #041431, pins and leader lines #0b1a38.

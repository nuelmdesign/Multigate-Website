03 · Payables Financing

Get your suppliers paid early. Keep your payment terms.

Eligible suppliers can be paid early by participating banks and lenders, on invoices you have approved and we have verified. You keep your payment terms. Your supply chain gets stronger.

Participating banks and lenders fund eligible invoices on the network.

How Payables Financing works
  1. 1

    Approve

    You approve supplier invoices as you do today.

  2. 2

    Verify

    We verify the approved invoice on the Multigate system.

  3. 3

    Offer and fund

    Banks and lenders offer terms. Suppliers who accept are paid early.

  4. 4

    Settle

    You pay on the original due date.

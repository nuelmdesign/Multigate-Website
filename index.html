The problem we solve

Running finance across Africashouldn't take ten bank portalsand a spreadsheet.

Managing money across multiple banks, business entities and markets shouldn't be complicated. Yet many finance teams still spend hours tracking balances, processing payments and reconciling transactions across disconnected systems.

Here are four challenges businesses face every day.

Cash visibility

“We can't easily see how much money we have.”

Your money is spread across different banks, accounts and currencies. Getting a complete picture means logging into multiple portals and pulling information together manually.

With Multigate

See all your money in one place.

View your cash balances across connected banks, business entities and currencies from one central dashboard.

Explore Treasury Management

Payments & collections

“Managing payments takes too much time.”

Payments require multiple steps, approvals happen over email, and incoming transfers can be difficult to identify. Your finance team spends too much time chasing transactions instead of managing finances.

With Multigate

Manage payments and collections from one place.

Approve and send payments through connected banks, while incoming payments are automatically matched to the right customer and invoice.

Explore Payments & Collections

Cross-border payments

“Sending money across African markets is complicated.”

Working across different countries often means dealing with multiple providers, unclear exchange rates and delays before money reaches its destination.

With Multigate

Collect, convert and send money across markets.

Access local collections, clearly quoted FX rates and payments in NGN, GHS and XOF through one connection.

Explore Payments & FX

Supplier financing

“Our suppliers can't afford to wait for payment.”

Long payment cycles can leave suppliers waiting weeks or months for money they've already earned. Many have to borrow at high interest rates just to keep their businesses running.

With Multigate

Give eligible suppliers access to early payments.

Suppliers can receive early payment from participating banks and lenders on invoices you've already approved, while your original payment terms remain unchanged.

03 · Payables Financing

Get your supplierspaid early.Keep your terms.

Give eligible suppliers access to early payment from participating banks and lenders on invoices you've already approved.

Your payment terms remain unchanged, while your suppliers get access to the cash they need to keep their businesses moving.

  1. 01 · Approve

    Approve supplier invoices as you normally would.

  2. 02 · Verify

    Multigate verifies the approved invoice and makes it available to participating financiers.

  3. 03 · Offer & fund

    Participating financiers provide financing offers. Suppliers choose whether to accept and receive early payment.

  4. 04 · Settle

    You pay on the original invoice due date. Your payment terms remain unchanged.

Early payments are funded by participating banks and lenders. Multigate does not lend.

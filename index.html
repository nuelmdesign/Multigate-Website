The problem

Your money is everywhere. Your view of it isn't.

Five banks means five portals, five statement formats and five versions of the truth. Balances are right at nine and wrong by noon. Payments leave on time and arrive whenever. Most finance teams in Africa still run treasury on spreadsheets, phone calls and patience. That is fragmentation, and it costs more than anyone can see.

Job of this section
The section that wins the banks. Neutrality, stated as three commitments.
How we operate

We never compete with the people we serve.

I.

We don't lend. Financiers do.

Multigate originates and operates trade finance programmes. The lending decision and the balance sheet stay with the banks and lenders who fund them.

II.

Banks keep their clients.

We connect corporates to their banks, never around them. The relationship stays where it started, and gets stronger.

III.

Your funds stay with licensed banks.

Collections and balances are held at licensed partner banks in each market we serve.

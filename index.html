Running finance across Africa shouldn't take ten bank portals and a spreadsheet.

03 · Payables Financing

Get your suppliers paid early. Keep your terms.

Give eligible suppliers early payment from participating banks and lenders on invoices you've already approved, so their businesses keep moving while your payment terms stay unchanged.

Early payments are funded by participating banks and lenders. Multigate does not lend.

Our commitment

Your relationships. Your choice. One connected network.

Multigate connects your financial operations without taking control away from the institutions and relationships you already trust.

You choose your banks

The Treasury & Trade OS is bank-agnostic. You decide which banks you connect and how you use them.

Banks keep their clients

We connect businesses to their banks. We don't route around existing banking relationships.

Financiers carry the financing

Multigate provides the platform, not the loans. Participating banks and lenders provide the financing and carry the credit risk.

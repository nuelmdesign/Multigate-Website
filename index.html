Job of this section
The four problems finance teams bring us, in their own words, and how Multigate helps with each.
The problem we solve

Running finance across Africa shouldn't take ten bank portals and a spreadsheet.

These are the four problems finance teams bring to us most often. Here is how Multigate helps with each.

“We can't see all our cash.”

Balances spread across banks, group companies and currencies, rebuilt by hand in spreadsheets, and out of date by the time they are ready.

With Multigate

One live view of your cash across your connected banks, group companies and currencies.

“Payments and collections eat our time.”

Approvals chased by email, payments typed in bank by bank, and money that arrives with no sign of who sent it.

With Multigate

Approve and send payments from one place. Incoming money is matched to the payer and invoice automatically.

“Moving money across African markets is slow and costly.”

Several providers, unclear exchange rates and long waits for cross-border payments to settle.

With Multigate

One connection to collect, convert and pay out in NGN, GHS and XOF, with clear exchange-rate quotes and every transaction tracked in your cash view.

“Our suppliers are short of cash.”

Long payment terms leave suppliers borrowing at high rates while they wait to be paid.

With Multigate

Eligible suppliers can be paid early by participating banks and lenders, on invoices you have approved. Your payment terms stay the same.

03 · Payables Financing

Get your suppliers paid early. Keep your payment terms.

Eligible suppliers can be paid early by participating banks and lenders, on invoices you have approved and we have verified. You keep your payment terms. Your supply chain gets stronger.

Participating banks and lenders fund eligible invoices on the network.

How Payables Financing works

  1. Approve

    You approve supplier invoices as you do today.

  2. Verify

    We verify the approved invoice on the Multigate system.

  3. Offer and fund

    Banks and lenders offer terms. Suppliers who accept are paid early.

  4. Settle

    You pay on the original due date.

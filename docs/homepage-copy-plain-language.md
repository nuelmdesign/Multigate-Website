# Multigate homepage: plain-language copy

Draft for sign-off · 12 November 2026

Elsa's copy is the source of truth. This draft does not change her positioning, claims, headlines, vision or mission. It changes the words around them so a curious reader who is not a finance specialist can follow every line, and so the page speaks in Multigate's brand voice.

## How to read this

Each section shows Elsa's text, the proposed text, and a short note. "Same" means her wording is kept as written. Items marked **[Confirm]** are open and listed again at the end.

## Two calls I have made (reverse either one)

1. **"System" is what Multigate is. "Network" is who is connected.** Elsa's headline ("One network for trade") and vision stay word for word. Where she says "Treasury & Trade OS", this draft says "the Multigate system" after explaining it once.
2. **Three jurisdictions, as Elsa has them** (Nigeria, Canada, Mauritius). The earlier site copy also named Uganda. **[Confirm]** with Legal.

## Rules applied everywhere

- British English and sentence case. "Trust Centre", not "Trust Center". **[Confirm]** Elsa uses "Center".
- No em-dashes. "&" becomes "and".
- Plain words first, technical term second. "Banks and lenders" before "Financiers". "Large buyers" instead of "anchor buyers". "Payment companies and platforms" instead of "fintechs, PSPs".
- Standards and protocol names (ISO 20022, Swift MT and MX, FileAct, PCI DSS, L2BA) stay exactly as written, in small type or in the proof strip, never in a headline.
- No claim is added. Where Elsa's text is an absolute ("solves each of them"), it is softened to what we can defend.

Our Story

A Note from Our Founder

Multigate began with a simple belief: Africa’s businesses deserve financial infrastructure that is as ambitious as they are. But a company is not its software. It’s the people who decide, every day, how to do the work and how to treat the people we serve. So this is really a story about them.

​

When we started, we were a small team taking on a problem much bigger than us. We had to earn the trust of banks, regulators and some of the largest companies on the continent, and we had to do it from first principles. That shaped who we became. We hire people who would rather understand a problem properly than solve it quickly. People who own their work from the first conversation to the last detail.

​

People who treat a client’s money and a client’s time with the same care as their own. Today our team spans engineering, design, operations, compliance, partnerships and client service. Behind every payment that settles and every supplier who gets paid early, there is someone at Multigate who made it happen and cared enough to get it right.

Our core values

Five values that guide how we work and serve.

Treasury and trade run on trust. These five values guide how we build, how we make decisions and how we treat the businesses and institutions we work with.

They apply to our whole team, from engineering to customer support.

Customer Obsession

We start with our customers’ problems and build lasting relationships around solving them.

Excellence

We set a high standard for our work and keep raising it.

Agility & Innovation

We adapt quickly and bring fresh ideas to hard problems, without losing precision.

Trust & Accountability

We do what we say, act with integrity and own the outcome.

Empowerment & Ownership

Our team members lead in their own areas and take ownership of the results.
